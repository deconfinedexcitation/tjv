Overview: These lectures cover the fundamentals of quantum field theory and its applications to condensed matter physics. They also introduce the basic principles of quantum information theory, including quantum communication protocols and quantum algorithms.

Physics prerequisites: Two semesters of quantum mechanics at the graduate level. Classical mechanics including special relativity. Some experience with classical field theory (e.g., classical electrodynamics) may be useful.

Mathematical prerequisites: Linear algebra, ordinary differential equations, basic methods of real and complex analysis, basic theory of groups and rings. I highly recommend "Mathematics for physics" by Stone and Goldbart.

Textbook: We will not follow any textbook closely, but will sometimes refer to the required texts for exercises. A "*" denotes an advanced text.
